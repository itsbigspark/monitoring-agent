# Sources and provenance

This skill is independently authored for Bigspark. It incorporates general
editorial ideas rather than copying an upstream skill as a fork.

Sources reviewed on 2026-07-21:

- [harshaneel/humanize](https://github.com/harshaneel/humanize), MIT: informed
  the evidence-backed audit, voice-sample, and second-pass concepts. Detector
  optimisation, authorship scoring, forced punctuation, and forced sentence
  variation were deliberately excluded.
- [ahgraber/skills: good-prose](https://github.com/ahgraber/skills/tree/main/skills/good-prose),
  CC0-1.0: informed the structure-before-line-editing workflow, concrete language,
  and information-bearing headings.
- [jmagly/aiwg](https://github.com/jmagly/aiwg), MIT: informed the separation of
  technical, executive, tutorial, and conversational voice profiles.
- [Smithery ai-humanizer catalogue entry](https://www.skills.sh/smithery/ai/ai-humanizer):
  informed the review, rewrite, and combined output modes. Its upstream source and
  licence were not sufficiently verifiable during review, so no content was
  copied.
- [Google developer documentation style guide](https://developers.google.com/style/)
  and [Microsoft writing style guide](https://learn.microsoft.com/style-guide/):
  informed the plain-language, audience, consistency, and scannability rules.
- [Diataxis](https://diataxis.fr/): informed the separation of tutorial, how-to,
  reference, and explanation needs.

Source reviewed on 2026-08-03:

- [ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English](https://www.asd-ste100.org/):
  informed the controlled-English guidance for short instructions, consistent
  terminology, explicit actors, ordered procedures, and clear conditions. The
  skill adapts these principles to software engineering. It does not copy the
  controlled dictionary and does not claim formal ASD-STE100 compliance.

Maintain this file when a future change copies, adapts, or materially depends on
another source. Record the source, licence, reviewed revision or date, and which
ideas were accepted or rejected.
